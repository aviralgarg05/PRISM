# Ethics, exposure and release

This project optimises personas that move a language model's measured political position, and
some of what it produces is material people should not be handed casually. This note says what
the risks are, how anyone working on the project is protected, and what will and will not be
released. It is written before the labelling work in [FRAMING.md](FRAMING.md) starts, and it
applies to that work.

## What the project produces

- **Essays written in offensive personas.** The hand-written library in `code/utils/roles.py`
  includes personas named after Hitler and Stalin, and personas called `facist`, `extremist`,
  `radred` and `badhuman`. Every enumeration writes 62 essays per persona. On gemma3 alone the
  corpus holds hundreds of essays in these personas.
- **A persona that flips a model into endorsing racial superiority.** Optimising for an
  authoritarian coordinate produced a persona under which the model argues that "our race
  possesses many superior qualities compared to other races" and that different sorts of
  people should keep to their own kind, where the seed persona had called the same claim
  morally wrong (§27). This was a stance flip and not a refusal bypass: the model complied
  both times, once by rejecting the claim and once by endorsing it (§27).
- **A record of which statements move a model.** 18 of 62 statements carry all of one measured
  gain and two of them carry 24% (§27). That is, in effect, a short list of where a model's
  expressed position is easiest to move.

## Exposure protocol for reading essays

Anyone who reads generated essays as part of this project, including the author, follows this:

1. **Know before you read.** Anyone asked to label or audit essays is told in advance which
   personas are in the sample, is shown the statement list, and is told that the sample
   contains racist, antisemitic and authoritarian content generated by a model.
2. **Consent and opt-out.** Nobody is asked to read this material without agreeing to it
   first, and anyone may stop at any point without giving a reason and without the work being
   reassigned as a favour.
3. **Bounded sessions.** Labelling is done in sessions of at most one hour, with the sample
   drawn so that the most extreme personas are spread across sessions rather than concentrated
   in one.
4. **No naive or unpaid readers.** No crowdworkers, no students recruited casually, and no
   task posted without the warning above. If the labelling is ever paid work, it is paid at a
   normal rate and the exposure is described in the task.
5. **Redaction in writing.** Quotations in the paper are the shortest that make the point,
   are marked as model output, and are not reproduced in full. No figure reproduces an essay
   from the `hilter`, `facist` or `extremist` personas in full.

## Release policy

**Released with the paper:** the code, the refusal gate and its validation set, the scoring
analysis, the pre-registrations and outcomes, aggregate stance matrices and coordinates, and
the hand-written persona library, which is inherited from the original PRISM fork and is
already public.

**Not released:** the search-derived persona texts that reliably produce the stance flip
described above, and the full essay corpus for the offensive personas. These are the two
artefacts with a plausible misuse path, since a persona that moves a model's expressed
position is directly reusable as a jailbreak-adjacent prompt.

**Available on request**, to named researchers, for replication: the withheld persona texts and
essays, with the exposure note above attached.

## Dual use

The result that search can move a model's measured position is dual use in the ordinary way:
it is the same finding whether it is read as an audit critique or as a recipe. The mitigation
is that the paper's claim is about the measurement, so nothing in it requires publishing the
strongest persona text. Where a number is needed, the number is given; where a text would only
serve as a recipe, it is withheld.

## Human subjects and data

No human subjects, no personal data, no user data. All text is model output. The gold labels
used for assessor validation come from the corpora the original work used, under their own
terms. The Political Compass propositions are quoted from a copyrighted web quiz and are used
as research material, which is also why the paper cannot treat the instrument as a validated
psychometric scale.

## Ongoing obligations

- If any labelling is added to the project, this note is attached to the task description.
- If a persona is found that moves a model further than anything recorded here, it is reported
  in aggregate and the text is withheld under the release policy above.
- If the work moves to a venue with its own ethics review, this note is the starting draft,
  not the finished statement.
